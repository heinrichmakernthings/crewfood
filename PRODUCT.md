# Crew Table
Browser-based private catering price calculator for Sam's festival crew catering.
Primary task: configure build (10 days, 50 people), live (3 days, 150 people), break (4 days, 50 people), split workers into groups and meal entitlements, then calculate food, labour, consumables and margin.
Core crew and volunteers default to three meals. An additional editable group may receive two meals. Group splits are illustrative and clearly labelled. Users can compare all-two versus all-three meal pricing without overwriting their mixed plan.
Ten standard meals reuse ingredients. Recipes and Booker UK pack prices are editable. Source links and checked dates distinguish actual public prices from estimates. Preserve raw quantities, drained yields, whole pack rounding and traceable costing.
Assumptions: owner-only site, browser-local persistence with JSON export/import, no live supplier sync, desktop-first responsive UI. Costed menu mix is a budgeting mix rather than a dated service rota. Prices exclude recoverable VAT and selling prices are before VAT.
Visual requirement: easy UI. Use conventional legible operational controls with an immediately visible quote, not a marketing page.
